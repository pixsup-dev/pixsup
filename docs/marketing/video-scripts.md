# Pixsup: 3 short videos

For TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. Each one is 15–30 seconds.

**Before you film**
- Film on your iPhone with Pixsup open in Safari. Use the built-in screen recorder: swipe down from the top right, then tap ⏺.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb so no messages pop up.
- Use real posts. Have 2 or 3 friends online at the same time so you have posts to save and people saving yours.
- Put the words on screen as text in the TikTok or Instagram editor. Most people watch without sound.
- Add a trending sound at low volume behind your voice.

## Video 1: "Save it before it dies" (15–20 sec)

**The idea:** a post is about to vanish forever, and one tap saves it.

| Time | On screen | Text on screen | You say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–2 s | The 🚨 About to die row, a post with under a minute left and the timer counting down | **This photo dies in 40 seconds** | "This photo is about to be deleted forever." |
| 2–6 s | Tap the post to open it full screen. The red life bar is almost empty. | **Nobody can save it… except you** | "On this app, every post dies unless people keep it alive." |
| 6–9 s | Double-tap the photo. The ⚡ hit animation plays and the timer jumps back to 1 hour. | **SAVED ⚡ +1 hour** | "One tap, and it's saved." |
| 9–13 s | Close it. The post now shows "Saved by @you". | **Saved by @you** | "And your name goes on it." |
| 13–18 s | Scroll Home: the trending belt, World Pulse, the feed | **pixsup.com** | "It's called Pixsup. Go save something." |

**Caption:** This photo had 40 seconds to live 😳 pixsup.com #pixsup #savethepost #newapp

| Time | On screen | Text on screen | You say |

## Tips

- **The first 2 seconds decide everything.** Start right on the countdown or the dead post, with no intro.
- **Post one video a day** at the same time, ideally around your launch night. Post the same video on TikTok, Reels and Shorts.
- **Reply to comments with new videos.** If someone asks "what happens if nobody saves it?", film the answer.
- **Pin your best one** to the top of your profile.
- **Put pixsup.com in your profile bio** on every app.
